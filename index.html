About Prasobh Kumar

Dedicated engineering undergraduate blending mathematical rigor, automotive mechanics, and aerospace enthusiasm.

// PRASOBH KUMAR
Engineering Precision
Mechanical • Auto • Aero

"Engineering is the relentless pursuit of perfection in motion, structure, and physics."

I am currently in my 1st year pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in Mechanical Engineering at Government Engineering College Thrissur (GECT). Born and raised in Trivandrum, Kerala, I completed my Class 10 education at Loyola School, Mukundara and Class 12 at Christ Nagar School, Kuchapuram.

My engineering curiosities span across Automobile Engineering (powertrain kinematics, chassis dynamics, active aerodynamics), Aerospace Propulsion (gas turbine cycles, rocket thrust chambers), and Aeronautical UAV Systems (winglet efficiency, low-Reynolds flow, autonomous stability).

Mechanical & CAD

Parametric 3D assemblies, orthographic blueprints, and GD&T tolerances.

Automobile Engineering

Vehicle dynamics, epicyclic gear transmissions, and active aero surfaces.

Aerospace & Aero

Turbofan compressor stages, NACA airfoils, and boundary layer airflow.

Robotics & Sensors

Inverse kinematics, microcontrollers, and precision servo actuation.
